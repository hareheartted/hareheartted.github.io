School

Originally coming to George Mason University as a computer science student with dreams of fullstack design, Betty Phan has made a few stark changes to that path in her academic career.

After a few years of dabbling in Computer Science, Phan decided the field and career weren't what she wanted to do with her life. She instead made the switch to the Costello College of Business, concentrating on Finance, and picked up a minor in English to fulfill two passions in her life: law and writing.

Hoping to professionally pursue the track of becoming a fiscal corporate attorney, Phan tailors her cirriculum around classes that deal both in the legal and financial worlds of business.

English-wise, Phan hopes to turn her creative writing hobby into something serious, taking classes to hone her proclivity for creative fiction and poetry.

Phan will graduate from the Costello College of Business in the Spring of 2027.
