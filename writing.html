Creative Writing

Phan has a long, storied history with creative writing. She got her start with running a digital magazine in high school as its editor-in-chief, Phan continued to cultivate her talent through small projects and portfolios.

She is largely self-taught, though strives to improve her talent through critique in classes and online forums. She specializes in fiction and poetry.

Currently, she is working on getting published in the online magazine glut.

A brief excerpt from seabed (lay me to rest)

For years now, I've had the same dream: overlooking a sea of evergreen peaks illuminated by a silky moonlight hue, winding a long, narrow road darkened on both sides by tightly clustered trees, my arms wrapped around my chest, cold layered like a skin, sinking deep into my bones, cold wet like a damp hate; trees clustered around my wandering soul, spurned into wandering like a little undead ghost with teeth clattering in Morse code, saying: so many wrongs done, it is almost incomprehensible.

Is it too much to ask to be wanted?

Current Works-In-Progress

Currently, Phan finds herself occupied with the creation of a full poetry portfolio as well as a myriad of stories she hopes will grow into full-fledged novels.

She is most interested in fantasy settings and horror undertones. Current projects on her docket include a supernatural western fantasy and an American south gothic cult horror.
